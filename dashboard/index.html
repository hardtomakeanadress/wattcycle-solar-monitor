Details & chart help

Move across or tap a chart to inspect the nearest reading. Earlier and Later move by one window. Latest follows incoming readings at the current zoom. Times use your browser’s local timezone. Solar readings are normally collected every 5 minutes and climate readings every 10 minutes; climate readings occur every 5 minutes while heating is armed. Older solar history keeps its original one-minute detail; all readings are plotted at their actual times. Zoom cannot reveal measurements between readings. Climate history may take about 5 minutes to appear after a live reading.

Solar input is panel voltage; charging power is the controller’s output to the battery. The controller’s solar-cycle energy and peak reset with morning light and remain visible overnight, including after midnight. Long ranges show representative readings, so brief peaks and state changes may be omitted. All saved readings, including older minute readings, stay in storage.
